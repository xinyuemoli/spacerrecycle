A disk cleaner you can actually trust.

SpaceRecycle tells you where your disk space went, lets you pick what to clean, and asks you to confirm — it never deletes anything on its own.

Windows 10 1809+ · ~10 MB · open source (MIT) · no ads, no bundleware, no background services

Where your space actually went

🧹 Temp junk & caches

Temp folders, browser caches, crash dumps, and Windows Update leftovers — typically 1–20 GB, safe to reclaim.

📦 Dev artifacts

node_modules, target, .venv, and package caches — usually the biggest win, 10–100+ GB.

📁 Large files

Files over 500 MB, aggregated by directory so you see who is eating the space — then decide for yourself.

🗑️ App leftovers

Directories left behind by uninstalled apps and stale installers, cross-checked against what's actually installed.

Built on trust, not tricks

  • Nothing is preselected. Every action starts with you.
  • Preview before anything happens. You see exactly what will be purged or quarantined, grouped by category.
  • Quarantine, not deletion. Unsure items move to an NTFS-compressed, restorable store — they are only destroyed when you say so.
  • Audit log. Every cleanup, restore, and purge is written to a traceable log.
  • Open source. The source is public under MIT — read it, build it yourself, and verify there is no silent cleanup.
