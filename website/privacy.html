Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

SpaceRecycle is built around a simple promise: it never does anything on your device without you asking. This page explains exactly what that means for your data — including the two optional features that talk to Microsoft on your behalf.

What the app does on your machine

  • No telemetry. The app sends no usage data, analytics, or crash reports anywhere.
  • No account needed. Scanning, cleanup and the local quarantine work with no sign-in of any kind. The optional OneDrive features below are the only thing that ever asks you to sign in.
  • Scanning, cleanup, the quarantine store and the audit log all happen locally. They are written to your own disk and sent nowhere.
  • We run no server. There is nothing of ours for the app to report to, and your file names, paths and usage are never transmitted to us.

The files the app creates live on your own disk: the quarantine store (default %ProgramData%\SpaceRecycle\Quarantine), the audit log, and its config file. You can inspect, export, or delete all of them at any time.

Optional: connecting your OneDrive

Cloud backup and OneDrive quarantine let you keep an item in your own OneDrive instead of on this disk. Nothing here happens unless you connect an account yourself, and you can disconnect at any time from Settings.

  • Limited permission. The app asks for Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder. That grants access to one folder of its own inside your OneDrive and nothing else — it cannot read, list, or change any other file you have there.
  • Cloud quota. It reads how much space your OneDrive has left, so it can show that number and check an upload will fit.
  • Uploads go to your own OneDrive. When you choose to back an item up or quarantine it to the cloud, the files are uploaded to Apps/SpaceRecycle Backup/ in your drive.
  • We never receive any of it. The transfer is between your device and Microsoft's servers. We have no server, no copy, and no way to read your files.
  • The sign-in credential stays on your machine, encrypted with Windows DPAPI in %APPDATA%\SpaceRecycle\auth.bin. Disconnecting the account deletes it.

Subscription checks

Some features require a subscription, bought through the Microsoft Store. The app asks the Store whether your subscription is active; Microsoft handles that check and the payment, and we never see your payment details. The only thing kept locally is whether the subscription was valid and when it was last checked.

This website

The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookie-less, privacy-first analytics service. It reports aggregate page views and visits only — no cookies, no fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking, and no personal data stored. The site is fully static: no accounts, no forms, no comments.

Downloads

Installers are served by GitHub Releases. GitHub may record the download under GitHub's Privacy Statement. We only ever see an aggregate download count.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. The app has no mechanism for sending us data at all.

Changes
